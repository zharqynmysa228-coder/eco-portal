Eco-Tips

Rinse before you sort

Clean bottles and jars keep the rest of the paper and plastic recyclable.

Carry your own bag

One reusable bag replaces hundreds of plastic ones over its life.

Flatten cardboard

Flat boxes take less space in the container and in the truck.
